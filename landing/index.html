Saviour

A personal spend tracker that runs entirely on your Mac. Your data never leaves your machine.

What it does

Saviour reads HDFC transaction alerts from your Gmail, categorises them, and shows a clean monthly dashboard — total spent vs budget, where the money's going, top expenses, anything that needs review.

1Get a Gmail app password

Saviour reads your bank's transaction emails. To do that securely, Google gives you a one-time 16-character "app password" that's separate from your real Gmail password. You can revoke it any time.

How to get one (~2 min):

  1. Open the link below.
  2. If 2-Step Verification isn't on yet, turn it on first (link will guide you).
  3. Type Saviour as the app name, click Create.
  4. Copy the 16-character password Google shows you.

3Run the installer

Open Terminal on your Mac (Cmd+Space → "Terminal" → Enter), then paste the command below and hit Enter. It will:

  • Download Saviour to ~/saviour
  • Install the few Python packages it needs
  • Save your config (locally, on your Mac)
  • Pull your last 90 days of HDFC alerts and parse them

Total time: ~3-5 minutes.

Step A — download the installer:

Step B — run it in Terminal:

bash ~/Downloads/saviour-install.sh

Step C — open your dashboard:

Once the installer says "Saviour is installed!", run this in the same Terminal window:

cd ~/saviour && python3 src/app.py

Then visit http://localhost:5001 in your browser. You're done.

To open Saviour again on a future day, just run Step C — your data and config are saved. The dashboard has a ⟳ Sync button that pulls fresh transactions any time you click it.
